Our products

Three products. One commitment to making your day easier.

adXpert, ctvXpert and erpXpert — built over more than a decade, refined by daily use in advertising agencies, cable TV networks and billing departments across India.

Product 01

adXpert

Software for advertising agencies.

adXpert manages the full cycle of an advertising agency — from receiving release orders, booking insertions, generating publisher and client bills, tracking outstandings, all the way to posting accounting entries into Tally.

RO

Release order management

Single and multi-insertion ROs, with release date checking, special rates, design charges and split bills.

B

Client & publisher billing

Auto-generated bills, drill-down to source orders, PDF and Excel export, e-mail directly from adXpert.

A

Accounting & Tally integration

Vouchers posted to Tally with a single click, with ledger drill-down and creditor-wise reports.

AI

AI assistance new

RO extraction from emails & PDFs, rate-card matching, and voice-driven bookings via the AI media server.

Product 02

ctvXpert

Software for cable TV providers.

ctvXpert handles the entire back-office of a cable TV network: subscribers, packages, recharges, complaints, collections, multi-branch operations and accounting — all in one place.

S

Subscriber & package management

Connections, pack changes, suspensions, transfers and address changes — with full audit trails.

R

Recharges & collections

Counter and field-collection workflows, with offline data sync from the Android app.

T

Multi-branch & Tally

Multi-branch and multi-company support, with accounting entries pushed automatically to Tally.

Product 03

erpXpert

Software for the billing section.

erpXpert is a focused ERP for businesses where billing is the heartbeat — quotations, sales orders, invoices, receipts, credit limits, ageing analysis and a clean integration with Tally.

Q

Quotations & orders

From quotation to sales order to invoice — clean status tracking and approvals at every step.

R

Receivables & ageing

Client-wise and age-wise outstanding, credit limits, payment follow-up reports.

X

Export & integrations

One-click export to PDF/Excel, with accounting vouchers posted to Tally automatically.
