In the lab

AI media server — publish ads by chat or by voice.

Our next-generation media server lets users add, schedule and publish ads using natural text chat and voice commands. No deep menus, no training overhead — just say what you want, confirm, and go live.

  • Conversational ad creation in plain English / regional languages
  • Voice-driven booking, edits and approvals
  • Auto-generation of release orders, invoices and Tally entries
  • Realtime monitoring of plays, reach and revenue
