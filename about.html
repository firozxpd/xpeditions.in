Who we are

Software professionals,
building useful things since 2008.

Xpeditions is a private limited company headquartered in Trivandrum, Kerala. We design and build software that makes other people's daily work simpler — and now we're extending that same approach to AI.

Introduction

Our story.

Xpeditions started as a partnership firm in December 2008 — the dreams of a small group of software professionals taking physical form. Today we are Xpeditions IT Solutions Pvt Ltd, a registered company based in Trivandrum, with clients across India and overseas.

We automate daily business workflows using open-source technologies and modern frameworks, minimizing manual effort by getting systems to talk to each other instead of relying on people to copy-paste between them. Our work spans enterprise applications, Tally integrations, Android apps, website development, open-source customization — and increasingly, AI.

Vision

Why we do it.

Faster execution yields better results. We design systems that communicate with each other — and with humans — to reduce the execution time of your workflow. Our goal is simple: reduce operational cost, improve responsiveness, and free your team to focus on the work that actually grows the business.

Alliance

Working with others.

We see strategic alliances as one of the most effective ways to diffuse new technologies and enter new markets quickly. Creating sustainable value for our customers and shareholders means finding the right partners — and we have been fortunate to find a few.

Our long-running alliance with Cenique Infotainment Group (Hong Kong), a company focussed on transforming retail through computer vision and anonymous video analytics, has been particularly fruitful — bringing IntelliAd, AndiStream and related platforms within reach for the Indian market.
