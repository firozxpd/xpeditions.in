What we serve

Services we offer.

Seven service lines, refined over fifteen years — from consultancy and application development to AI-powered automation and Tally integrations.

01

Consultancy Services

Before we write a line of code, we listen. We audit your existing processes, identify bottlenecks, and recommend whether the right answer is custom software, an off-the-shelf product, or simply a process change. Our goal is to save you from building things you don't need.

02

Application Development

Custom web and enterprise applications built on PHP, Java, Python and modern JavaScript frameworks. We focus on maintainability — code your future self, or your next vendor, will be happy to work with.

03

Tally Integration

One of our specialities. We bridge your operational software — orders, invoices, receipts, payments — directly with Tally, eliminating double-entry and the inevitable mismatches it causes. We handle vouchers, masters, GST, multi-branch and multi-company setups.

04

Android Solutions

Native and hybrid Android applications for field sales, dealers, delivery teams and customers. Offline-first where it matters, integrated with your backend wherever possible.

05

Open-Source Customization

Magento, WordPress, OpenCart, OFBiz, ERPNext, Odoo — we customize and extend popular open-source platforms to match how you actually work, rather than forcing your team to match the platform.

06

Website Development

Responsive, fast-loading websites that represent your business well — and integrate cleanly with the rest of your stack (CRM, ERP, marketing tools).
